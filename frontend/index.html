Our data sources

SemaCheck is not a black box. Here's exactly what powers every verdict.

Community scam database

Thousands of verified scam reports submitted by real Kenyans — Paybills, phone numbers, and job offers that people have already flagged.

CBK licensed lender registry

We cross-reference every Paybill against the Central Bank of Kenya's official list of licensed deposit-taking institutions.

Live web search + global databases

We run real-time web searches across government scam alerts (DCI, CBK), Kenyan news outlets, FTC, BBB Scam Tracker, and Interpol fraud alerts.

Transparency: SemaCheck is a research and reference tool — it is not insurance or a guarantee. A "clean" result means we found no reports at the time of the search. Always apply your own judgement. Read our full Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
