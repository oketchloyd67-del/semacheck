The SemaCheck Verified programme

What a verified badge means, how to get one, and how verdicts are built and contested.

What “Verified” means

A Verified badge means SemaCheck has seen and re-checked an organisation's business registration documents by hand, and that the paybill, till or phone numbers carrying the badge are registered to that verified organisation. It answers one question: “does this business really exist and own these numbers?”

What it does not mean: verification is not an endorsement of the business's conduct, not a guarantee that payments to it are safe, and not insurance. Verdicts remain point-in-time signals — always apply your own judgement before sending money.

How a verified verdict is built

  1. Registration check. Our admin team reviews the organisation's certificate of registration (and, for job postings, the KRA PIN) manually — no automated pass.
  2. Registry cross-check. Paybills and business names are matched against public records, including the CBK Digital Credit Providers registry where applicable.
  3. Report scan. The subject is scanned against every approved scam report in our database and live public web results.
  4. Verdict. The verdict (registered, unverified, caution or reported — the same four values the website, the docs and the API all use) is published with its reasons and confidence, and re-checked whenever a new report lands.

Every verdict shows the date it was built. A verdict can change — check again any time.

For businesses

Kenyan businesses can apply to have their numbers verified:

  • Submit your certificate of registration and the numbers (paybill, till, phone) you own through the contact form.
  • Our team reviews documents by hand, usually within 2 working days.
  • Verified organisations get the badge on their public report entries and can dispute any inaccurate report with priority review.
  • API partners verify their organisation during API signup (see the API dashboard).

Think a verdict is wrong?

Verdicts are built from reports and web evidence anyone can see — and reports can be wrong or out of date. If a verdict about your number, paybill or business is inaccurate:

  • Open the dispute page and submit a dispute with proof of ownership — upheld disputes correct the verdict everywhere, including the API.
  • Use the Wrong verdict? button shown under any search result to flag it to our review team.
