Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

SemaCheck ("SemaCheck", "we", "us", "our") operates a platform that helps people in Kenya verify whether a Paybill number, phone number, or job offer is legitimate. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we protect it, and what rights you have over it.

This policy is written to comply with the Data Protection Act, 2019 (the "DPA"), the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, and the Data Protection (Registration of Data Controllers and Data Processors) Regulations, 2021, all issued under the laws of Kenya and enforced by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner ("ODPC").

In short: we collect what we need to verify your identity, let you search, and process your M-Pesa payments — nothing more. We don't sell your data. You can ask us what we hold, correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time via the contact form.

We do not collect your M-Pesa PIN, full card numbers, or any payment credential beyond your phone number — payments are authorised directly between you and Safaricom's M-Pesa system.

2. Why we collect it (lawful basis)

  • To create and secure your account — identity and contact data, under our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and duplicate accounts, and to perform our contract with you (providing the service you signed up for).
  • To verify your identity — your national ID number and the ID document photo you upload are reviewed manually by an authorised member of our team (not by an automated system) so that accounts on the platform can be trusted, based on your consent given at sign-up.
  • To process payments — phone number and transaction data, necessary to perform the contract (delivering the search result or subscription you paid for).
  • To deliver search results — your query is checked against our database and, where configured, an external web search, to fulfil the service you requested.
  • To review job postings — business registration and KRA PIN, so our admin team can confirm a job owner is a real, traceable business before a posting goes live, protecting other users from fraudulent listings.
  • To respond to your enquiries — contact form submissions, based on your consent when you submit the form.
  • To comply with the law — where we're required to retain or disclose records (e.g. under the DPA, tax law, or a lawful order from a Kenyan court or regulator).

3. Sensitive personal data

Under Section 2 of the DPA, your national ID number and certain financial information are treated as sensitive personal data and given extra protection. We collect your national ID number solely to reduce impersonation and fraudulent accounts on the platform. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not share it with third parties except where the DPA or another law requires or permits it (see Section 4).

4. Who we share data with

  • Safaricom PLC — your phone number and payment amount, to process M-Pesa STK Push payments. Safaricom's own privacy practices govern how they handle this.
  • Our hosting and infrastructure providers — who store data on our behalf under a data processing arrangement, and are contractually required to protect it and not use it for their own purposes.
  • Email/SMS delivery providers — limited to what's needed to deliver a message you triggered (e.g. a contact-form confirmation).
  • The Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, courts, or law enforcement — only where we are legally required to disclose information.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

6. How we protect your data

  • Passwords are hashed (never stored or transmitted in plain text)
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Login sessions are tracked server-side, so logging out immediately and permanently invalidates that session — including on shared or public computers
  • Access to the database and admin tools is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • Uploaded ID document photos are never served by a public URL — they can only be viewed by a logged-in admin reviewing that specific account
  • We apply rate limiting and other technical safeguards against automated abuse and unauthorised access attempts

No system is 100% secure, but we apply appropriate technical and organisational measures as required under Section 41 of the DPA.

7. Your rights under the DPA

As a data subject under Kenyan law, you have the right to:

  • Be informed — how your data is used (this policy)
  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data, subject to our legal retention obligations (e.g. payment records under tax law)
  • Object / restrict processing — object to certain uses of your data, or ask us to limit how we use it
  • Data portability — request your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time (this may limit what parts of the service we can still offer you)
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, if you believe we have mishandled your data (odpc.go.ke, complaint@odpc.go.ke)

8. Data transferred outside Kenya

Where our hosting, email, or search infrastructure providers store or process data outside Kenya, we only use providers that offer an adequate level of data protection, and we take reasonable steps to ensure any cross-border transfer complies with Section 48 of the DPA and its regulations.

9. Cookies & session storage

We use browser session storage (not tracking cookies) to keep you logged in during a browsing session. This data is cleared automatically when you close your browser, and immediately invalidated server-side the moment you log out.

10. Children's data

SemaCheck is not intended for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

11. Data breach notification

In the event of a data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ODPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and notify affected users without undue delay, in line with Section 43 of the DPA.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes we'll make reasonable efforts to notify active users directly.

13. How to contact us / exercise your rights

To access, correct, or delete your data, or for any other privacy question, use the contact form on our homepage, or write to us at the email address listed there. We aim to respond within 14 days.

This policy is a general compliance document and does not constitute legal advice. If you have specific legal concerns about how your data is handled, you're welcome to raise them via the contact form, or seek independent legal advice.
