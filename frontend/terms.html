1. What SemaCheck does

SemaCheck helps you verify whether a Paybill number, phone number, or job offer is likely legitimate. We do this by:

  • Cross-referencing your query against our own database of community-reported scams and consumer alerts
  • Checking Kenya's Central Bank licensed deposit-taking institutions registry
  • Running a live web search across public scam databases, consumer complaint records, and international fraud alerts (including FTC, BBB, and Interpol sources)
  • Analysing patterns in job-offer text to flag known scam indicators

Every result is a best-effort assessment based on publicly available information at the time of the search. It is not a legal opinion, financial guarantee, or law-enforcement determination.
