API terms of use

These terms apply to anyone using the SemaCheck Trust Check API (the “Service”). By creating an API account or calling the API, you agree to these terms.

1. What the Service is

The Service is an automated scam and fraud verification tool. It analyses a phone number, paybill, business name, website, or job offer and returns a verdict, a confidence score, and reasons. It is information, not legal advice.

2. Acceptable use

You may not use the API for:

  • Harassment, stalking, or discrimination.
  • Compiling a list of personal phone numbers or business data for sale or redistribution.
  • Re-selling raw verdicts or search results without permission.
  • Any unlawful purpose or activity that violates Kenyan law or the Data Protection Act 2019.
  • Automated probing, scraping, or denial-of-service of the API.

3. Verdicts are informational

Verdicts are rules-based signposts, not legal findings. They are not a guarantee that a subject is or is not a scam. A “reported” verdict means there is evidence worth checking further, not that a subject is fraudulent. An “unverified” verdict means we have no evidence either way. You are responsible for your own decisions.

4. Plans, billing, expiry and grace period

Each plan has a monthly price, a monthly quota of live checks, and a rate limit. The Free plan is a sandbox plan: it issues test keys only and includes no live checks. Paid plans are billed monthly in advance by M-Pesa. An unpaid invoice makes the account past_due on the renewal date; you then have a 7-day grace period during which live keys keep working. After the grace period the account is suspended and live keys answer HTTP 402 until the invoice is settled — 402 always means “pay or wait”, never a bad key. Sandbox test keys keep working during a billing suspension; a closed account blocks every key. Renewal reminders are emailed 14, 7, 3 and 1 days before each renewal date, on the due date, then 3 and 6 days into the grace period, followed by a suspension notice. After any downtime you receive only the latest reminder that is due — never a burst of them.

5. Service levels

We target 99% monthly availability of the check API for paid plans, excluding scheduled maintenance, third-party outages (including M-Pesa and external search providers), and events outside our reasonable control. Live availability is published on our status page. At current pricing we do not offer service credits; persistent availability problems are handled case by case in good faith, and you may terminate your plan for the unfulfilled remainder.

6. Key security duties

API keys authenticate your account. You must:

  • Keep secret keys (sk_…) server-side only — never in browser code, mobile apps, public repositories, or client-visible logs.
  • Store keys hashed or encrypted at rest, and never transmit them in plaintext outside TLS.
  • Rotate keys proactively (create a replacement, then revoke the old key) and revoke any key you believe is compromised.
  • Not share live keys outside your organisation or resell access to the API.
  • Tell us promptly of any unauthorised use or key leak so we can revoke access.

You are responsible for calls made with your keys. Publishable keys (pk_…) are designed for browsers and are locked to the domains you register.

7. Refunds

Once a check result has been delivered, that check is consumed and non-refundable. Where a paid plan cannot be used at all because of a fault on our side (for example a prolonged outage), contact us within 14 days and we will refund the unused part of that month's fee or extend your plan, at your choice. M-Pesa payments that fail or are cancelled before a result is delivered are never charged. Refunds are processed to the M-Pesa number that paid.

8. No re-sale of results

You may not republish raw verdict data, search snippets, or the public alerts feed as your own product without written permission. Cached and derived statistics are permitted for internal reporting.

9. Fair use — verdicts are not a basis to refuse a person

A verdict is a point-in-time signal about reported evidence, not a finding of guilt. You must not use a verdict as the sole basis to refuse an individual service, employment, housing, or credit, or to publish allegations identifying a private individual. Where a verdict affects a real person, apply human review, allow the person to respond, and use our dispute process so corrected verdicts reach your integration. Regulated decisions (credit, employment screening) may require additional checks under Kenyan law — our verdict is one input, not the decision.
