1. What the Service is

The Service is an automated scam and fraud verification tool. It analyses a phone number, paybill, business name, website, or job offer and returns a verdict, a confidence score, and reasons. It is information, not legal advice.

2. Acceptable use

You may not use the API for:

  • Re-selling raw verdicts or search results without permission.
  • Any unlawful purpose or activity that violates Kenyan law or the Data Protection Act 2019.
  • Automated probing, scraping, or denial-of-service of the API.

3. Verdicts are informational

Verdicts are rules-based signposts, not legal findings. They are not a guarantee that a subject is or is not a scam. A “reported” verdict means there is evidence worth checking further, not that a subject is fraudulent. An “unverified” verdict means we have no evidence either way. You are responsible for your own decisions.
